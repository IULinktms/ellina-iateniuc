I started photographing with a borrowed film camera and a habit of noticing how light falls across a room in the late afternoon. Years later, that habit is still the whole method.

I work across portraits, weddings, editorial and brand commissions, and I shoot film alongside stills so that each project can be told in both. My clients are people and companies who care more about how a picture feels than how loud it is.
