On 23 October, Casmara Moldova brought aesthetics professionals together in Chișinău. A guest speaker from Valencia presented the Nacar, Infinity and Ageda’s Secret Treatment protocols — a day about knowledge, innovation and practitioners learning from one another.

I photographed and filmed the whole day: guests at the branded photo wall, the certificate presentations and the team’s closing applause on stage.
