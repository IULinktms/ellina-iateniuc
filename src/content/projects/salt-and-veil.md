The brief was simple: no posing that would interrupt the day. I arrived the evening before to learn the light on the beach and the sound of the wind, so on the day itself I could stay out of the way.

Most of these frames were made in the twenty minutes between the ceremony and dinner, when the sun dropped under the cloud line and turned everything the colour of linen.
